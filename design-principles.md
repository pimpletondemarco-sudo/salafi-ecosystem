# SunnahSaplings design principles

Adapted for print from the MIT-licensed Taste Skill research (Leonxlnx/taste-skill: a study of 247
award-level sites). Only the parts that apply to printable workbooks, covers and listing images.
The owner's rules in CLAUDE.md always win.

## Avoid the "AI look" (the tells)
- **Cream ground plus terracotta/rust/clay accent is the most recognisable generated look of
  2025–26.** Warm off-white grounds are fine with a dark or green ink, not with an orange accent.
- No gradient-filled text, no glows, no soft grey drop shadow under every box.
- No tiny letter-spaced UPPERCASE labels scattered around ("BASED ON…", "CITED · VERIFIED").
  Sentence case at a readable size, placed with what it describes.
- No middle-dot strings ("Workbook · Coloring · …") and no pill badges as decoration.
- No hairline rules, dashed guides or thin 1px outlined boxes as ornament; use space, tone steps
  and filled shapes.
- No emoji as icons. No "01 / 02 / 03" numbering unless it is a real sequence.
- Removing tells is not enough: replace them with substance from the subject (the date palm and
  its roots, the actual Arabic words, real proofs).

## Colour
- Roles: ground (70–90%), surface (one tone step from ground), ink (text), muted (secondary text),
  one accent under 5% of the page. Headings are ink, not accent.
- Good fits for SunnahSaplings from the library: "Bone, green, chocolate" (#e5e1d8 / #2a1d17 /
  accent #456f3a), "Sand and moss" (#ddd6c6 / #2a2a26 / #4f6238), "Bottle green, gold"
  (#133621 / #f4d96c), "Olive on bone" (#ecebe7 / #38450f).

## Type
- Few sizes, far apart: one display, one text, one small (five at most). Display at least 4× body.
- Big type does not need to be bold. Tighten tracking as size grows (-0.02 to -0.04em above 40px).
- One or two families, each with one job. No third face for labels.
- Headlines about five words, never more than two lines; author the line break.
- Arabic: fully voweled, on a straight baseline, never squeezed against Latin text.

## Layout (print)
- Not everything centred: book covers may centre the title, but inside pages anchor left.
- One dominant element per page. Group related things; separate groups with space, not lines.
