# Lesson 1: The Four Things We Must Learn (المسائل الأربع)

Researched by: source-researcher · 2026-10-16 · Status: CHECKED (see VERIFICATION.md), fixes applied

## Why children learn this
- Arabic source text (Ibn Baz, footnote 2):
  «هذه المسائل يجب أن يتعلمها المؤمن والمؤمنة صغارا وكبارا.»
- Simple meaning: "Every believer, male and female, young and old, must learn these matters."
- Citation: Shaykh Ibn Baz, *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shamela book 11255, page 15 (printed p. 21) — https://shamela.ws/book/11255/15

## Base text (matn), as quoted in the explanations
- «اعْلمْ رَحِمَكَ اللهُ أَنَّهُ يَجِبُ عَلَيْنَا تَعَلُّمُ أربع مسائل»

## Simple meanings (ages 6–10)
1. **Knowledge** — knowing Allah, His Prophet ﷺ and the religion of Islam, with proofs.
   - Ibn Baz: «وهذا العلم هو معرفة الله ومعرفة نبيه ومعرفة دين الإسلام بالأدلة» and «قال الله وقال الرسول لا بالرأي ولا بقول فلان»
   - Simple meaning: "Knowledge means knowing Allah, His Prophet and Islam with proofs: 'Allah said' and 'the Messenger said', not by opinion, nor by the saying of so-and-so."
   - Citation: book 11255, page 16 (printed p. 22) https://shamela.ws/book/11255/16
2. **Acting on it** — doing what we learned.
   - Al-Uthaymeen: «والعمل في الحقيقة هو ثمرة العلم»
   - Simple meaning: "Action is the fruit of knowledge." (Pairs with tree artwork.)
   - Citation: book 11257, page 15 (printed p. 22)
3. **Calling others to it** — inviting others to what we learned, with knowledge.
   - Al-Uthaymeen: «ولا بد لهذه الدعوة من علم بشريعة الله عز وجل حتى تكون الدعوة عن علم وبصيرة»
   - Simple meaning: "Calling others must be with knowledge and clear insight."
   - Evidence quoted there: Surah Yusuf 12:108 and an-Nahl 16:125.
   - Citation: book 11257, page 16 (printed p. 22)
4. **Patience** — being patient when we are hurt or troubled because of it.
   - Al-Uthaymeen, the three kinds of patience:
     «١-صبر على طاعة الله. ٢-صبر عن محارم الله. ٣-صبر على أقدار الله»
   - Simple meaning: "Patience in obeying Allah, patience in staying away from what He forbade, and patience with what Allah decrees."
   - Citation: book 11257, page 20 (printed p. 25) https://shamela.ws/book/11257/20

## Evidence: Surat al-'Asr (103:1–3)
- As quoted by al-Uthaymeen: {وَالْعَصْرِ إِنَّ الْأِنْسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ}
- His explanation: «...إلا من أتصف بهذه الصفات الأربع: الإيمان، والعمل الصالح، والتواصي بالحق، والتوصي بالصبر.»
- Citation: book 11257, page 21 (printed p. 25) https://shamela.ws/book/11257/21
- NOTE for designer: print the ayat from a standard mushaf text (see owner question), not from
  this transcription (it has spelling variants, e.g. «الْأِنْسَانَ»).

## Activity ideas (no living beings)
- Four-root tree: label roots Knowledge / Action / Calling / Patience; the fruit is "Action is the fruit of knowledge".
- Match the matter to its Arabic word (العلم، العمل، الدعوة، الصبر).
- Trace Surat al-'Asr; memorization checklist with stars.
- "Which kind of patience?" sorting: praying on time / not taking what isn't yours / being patient when sick.
