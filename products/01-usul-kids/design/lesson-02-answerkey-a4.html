For parents: answers to Lesson 2, page 1 (Learn)

Answer Key: Allah Created Us

الأُصُولُ الثَّلَاثَةُ
Lesson 2, page 1, filled in.

1Trace the words

No single answer. Check that the child traced each word and its marks (harakat), then wrote it alone in the box (shown in green).

خَلَقَنَاHe created us
khalaqanā
رَزَقَنَاHe provided for us
razaqanā

2Fill in the missing word

purpose. The child circles "purpose" and writes it on the line: "Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose."

In Arabic:لَمْ يَتْرُكْنَا هَمَلًاlam yatruknā hamalā: He did not leave us without a purpose
Source for these answers.
Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abdul-Wahhab wrote in Thalathat al-Usul:
أَنَّ اللهَ خَلَقَنَا وَرَزَقَنَا ولم يتركنا هملاً
Meaning of his words: Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose.
Shaykh Ibn Baz explains:
الله خلق الخلق ليعبدوه
Meaning of Shaykh Ibn Baz's words: Allah created His creation to worship Him.
Both from Sharh Thalathat al-Usul, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz, Dar al-Masir, 1st ed., p. 29. Shaykh Ibn Baz quotes the words of Thalathat al-Usul and explains them on that page.
For parents. The small copy shows each answer in green. Tracing has no single answer.
