Answer Key: The Four Matters in Order

Lesson 1, page 2, filled in.

1Put the four matters in order

The pieces go in the numbered boxes in this order, the order of Thalathat al-Usul:

1الْعِلْمُKnowledge
2الْعَمَلُActing upon it
3الدَّعْوَةُCalling others to it
4الصَّبْرُPatience

Each piece sits in its numbered box. Any order other than 1, 2, 3, 4 above needs another try.
