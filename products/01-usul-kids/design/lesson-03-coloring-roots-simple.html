الأُصُولُ الثَّلَاثَةُ
The Three Fundamental Principles
Color the date palm. Then trace over each root!
Knowing my Lord, my religion and my Prophet ﷺ: these three principles gather the whole religion.
From Sharh Thalathat al-Usul: Shaykh Ibn Baz, p. 38, and Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen, p. 42 · Surah Ibrahim 14:24
