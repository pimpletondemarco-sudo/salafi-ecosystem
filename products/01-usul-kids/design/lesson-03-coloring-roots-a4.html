Name:
الأُصُولُ الثَّلَاثَةُ
The Three Fundamental Principles
Color the tree. Its roots are what every Muslim must know.
Shaykh al-Uthaymeen explains: "Usul" means foundations (principles), what other things are built on, like the root of a tree that the branches grow from.
أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ ضَرَبَ ٱللَّهُ مَثَلٗا كَلِمَةٗ طَيِّبَةٗ كَشَجَرَةٖ طَيِّبَةٍ أَصۡلُهَا ثَابِتٞ وَفَرۡعُهَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ ٢٤
Meaning: "See you not how Allâh sets forth a parable? A goodly word as a goodly tree, whose root is firmly fixed, and its branches (reach) to the sky (i.e. very high)." (Ibrahim 14:24, The Noble Qur'an, Hilali-Khan)
