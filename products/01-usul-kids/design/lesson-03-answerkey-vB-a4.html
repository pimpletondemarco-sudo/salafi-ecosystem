For parents: answers to the Lesson 3 worksheet

Answer Key: My Three Roots

الأُصُولُ الثَّلَاثَةُ

1Trace the words

No single answer. Check that the child traced each word and its marks (harakat), and wrote it alone in the box (shown in green).

رَبِّيMy Lord
rab-bee
دِينِيMy Religion
dee-nee
نَبِيِّيMy Prophet
na-bee-yee

2Match

رَبِّي→My Lord
دِينِي→My Religion
نَبِيِّي→My Prophet

3Fill in the missing word

The child circles religion and writes it on the line: "These three principles gather the whole religion."

The worksheet, filled in.
Source for this answer. Meaning of Shaykh Ibn Baz's words: "These three principles gather the whole religion." Sharh Thalathat al-Usul, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz, Dar al-Masir, 1st ed., p. 38.
