# Teaching Guide — Lesson 1: The Four Matters We Must Learn

*The Three Fundamental Principles (Thalathat al-Usul for Kids) · SunnahSaplings*
*Draft for owner review. Every scholar quote is a verified quote; English lines are meanings, not word-for-word translations.*

## Goal of the lesson
By the end, your child can name the four matters every Muslim must learn (knowledge, acting upon it, calling others to it, and patience with the harm that comes with it) and knows that Shaykh Ibn Baz said young and old must learn them.

## Background for the parent (2 minutes)
Shaykh al-Islam Muhammad ibn Abdul-Wahhab wrote *Thalathat al-Usul*. Shaykh Ibn Baz explains that the author used to teach these foundations to students and ordinary people, so that they would study them and memorise them (Ibn Baz, p. 21). Shaykh Ibn Baz and Shaykh al-Uthaymeen both explained the book.

The book begins with four matters we must learn:
1. **Knowledge**: knowing Allah, knowing His Prophet ﷺ and knowing the religion of Islam, with the proofs.
2. **Acting upon it**: doing what we learned.
3. **Calling others to it**: inviting others to what we learned, with knowledge.
4. **Patience** with the harm that comes on this path. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen explains that the one who calls to good may be hurt by people, by words and by actions, and must stay patient and keep going.

The proof the scholars quote is Surat al-'Asr. In this lesson your child learns the four matters; later lessons explain what we must know.

## From the Scholars (commentary)

**1. "We must learn four matters": the words of the original text, as quoted by Shaykh Ibn Baz**

> اعْلمْ رَحِمَكَ اللهُ أَنَّهُ يَجِبُ عَلَيْنَا تَعَلُّمُ أربع مسائل

Meaning: "Know, may Allah have mercy on you, that we must learn four matters."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 21

**2. Who must learn them? Shaykh Ibn Baz**

> هذه المسائل يجب أن يتعلمها المؤمن والمؤمنة صغارا وكبارا.

Meaning: "Every believer, male and female, young and old, must learn these matters."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 21

**3. The four matters, as headed by Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen**

> العلم · العمل به · الدعوة إليه · الصبر على الأذى فيه

Meaning: "Knowledge · acting upon it · calling others to it · patience with the harm that comes with it."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), pp. 19, 22, 22, 24

**4. What is the knowledge? Shaykh Ibn Baz**

> وهذا العلم هو معرفة الله ومعرفة نبيه ومعرفة دين الإسلام بالأدلة … قال الله وقال الرسول لا بالرأي ولا بقول فلان

Meaning: "This knowledge is knowing Allah, knowing His Prophet ﷺ and knowing the religion of Islam, with the proofs: 'Allah said' and 'the Messenger said', not by opinion and not by what so-and-so said."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 22

**5. Action is the fruit: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> والعمل في الحقيقة هو ثمرة العلم

Meaning: "Action, in truth, is the fruit of knowledge."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 22

**6. Calling others needs knowledge: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> ولا بد لهذه الدعوة من علم بشريعة الله عز وجل حتى تكون الدعوة عن علم وبصيرة

Meaning: "This calling must be with knowledge of the law of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, so that the calling is with knowledge and clear insight."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 22

**7. Patience with the harm: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> فعلى الداعية أن يكون صابراً على دعوته مستمراً فيها. صابراً على ما يعترضه هو من الأذى

Meaning: "So the one who calls to Allah must be patient in his calling and keep going with it, patient with whatever harm comes to him."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 24

**8. The three kinds of patience: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> ١-صبر على طاعة الله. ٢-صبر عن محارم الله. ٣-صبر على أقدار الله

Meaning: "1. Patience in obeying Allah. 2. Patience in staying away from what Allah forbade. 3. Patience with what Allah decrees."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 25

**9. The proof Shaykh al-Uthaymeen quotes: Surat al-'Asr 103:1–3**

> وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ ١ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ ٢ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ ٣

Meaning: "By Al-‘Asr (the time). Verily, man is in loss, Except those who believe (in Islâmic Monotheism) and do righteous good deeds, and recommend one another to the truth [i.e. order one another to perform all kinds of good deeds (Al-Ma‘ruf) which Allâh has ordained, and abstain from all kinds of sins and evil deeds (Al-Munkar which Allâh has forbidden], and recommend one another to patience (for the sufferings, harms, and injuries which one may encounter in Allâh’s Cause during preaching His religion of Islâmic Monotheism or Jihâd)." (*The Noble Qur'an*, Hilali-Khan, 103:1–3). Arabic text: King Fahd Complex (Madinah) mushaf.
Cited by Shaykh al-Uthaymeen in *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul* (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 25

## Say it like this (script)
1. "Today we learn four matters that every Muslim must learn. Shaykh Ibn Baz said every believer, young and old, must learn them. That means you too!"
2. "The first is **knowledge**: knowing Allah, knowing His Prophet ﷺ and knowing the religion of Islam, with the proofs: 'Allah said' and 'the Messenger said'."
3. "The second is **acting upon it**. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said action is the fruit of knowledge. A tree gives fruit; knowledge gives action."
4. "The third is **calling others to it**: telling others what we learned. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said we call others with knowledge."
5. "The fourth is **patience**. Sometimes people say unkind words or do unkind things to someone who calls to good. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said he stays patient and keeps going."
6. "Allah told us about these in Surat al-'Asr." Read the meaning of 103:1–3 together.
7. Worksheet: "Let's trace the four words, then match each one to its meaning (or write it), and finish the last activity." (Depending on the version: fill in the missing word "knowledge" in "Action is the fruit of knowledge", or circle who must learn these matters.)

## Say the Arabic words
| Arabic | Say it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| الْعِلْمُ | al-'ILM | knowledge |
| الْعَمَلُ | al-'A-mal | action (acting upon it) |
| الدَّعْوَةُ | ad-DA'-wah | calling others to it |
| الصَّبْرُ | as-SABR | patience |

(The ' in al-'ilm and al-'amal is the Arabic letter 'ayn, a sound from the throat. If you can't make it, say the vowel clearly.)

## If your child asks…
- **"Do kids have to learn this too?"** Yes. Shaykh Ibn Baz said every believer, male and female, young and old, must learn these matters (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 21).
- **"What kind of knowledge?"** Knowing Allah, His Prophet ﷺ and the religion of Islam, with the proofs: "Allah said" and "the Messenger said", not by opinion (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 22).
- **"Isn't knowing enough? Why do I have to do it?"** Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said action is the fruit of knowledge (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 22).
- **"Can I tell my friends?"** Yes, calling others is the third matter, and Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said it must be with knowledge (same book, p. 22). So tell them what you have really learned.
- **"What is patience?"** Shaykh al-Uthaymeen gave three kinds: patience in obeying Allah, patience in staying away from what Allah forbade, and patience with what Allah decrees (same book, p. 25).

## Review game: Four Roots
Write الْعِلْمُ, الْعَمَلُ, الدَّعْوَةُ and الصَّبْرُ on four paper "roots" under a paper tree. Say the English (knowledge / acting upon it / calling others to it / patience) and your child puts a paper leaf on the matching root. When all four are done, hang a paper fruit on the tree and say together: "Action is the fruit of knowledge."
