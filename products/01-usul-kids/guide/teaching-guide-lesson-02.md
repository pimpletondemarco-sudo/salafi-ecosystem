# Teaching Guide — Lesson 2: Allah Created Us

*The Three Fundamental Principles (Thalathat al-Usul for Kids) · SunnahSaplings*
*Draft for owner review. Every scholar quote is a verified quote; English lines are meanings, not word-for-word translations.*

## Goal of the lesson
By the end, your child can say the first of the three matters: Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose. Your child also knows that Allah created us to worship Him.

## Background for the parent (2 minutes)
After the four matters (Lesson 1), *Thalathat al-Usul* gives three matters that every Muslim, male and female, must learn and act upon. This lesson covers only the first one: Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose. The other two come in later lessons.

Shaykh Ibn Baz explains: Allah created His creation to worship Him; He did not create them without a purpose or for nothing. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen explains that the proof that Allah created us comes from the revelation and from reason: a person did not create himself, his parents did not create him, and he did not come by chance, so the Creator is Allah alone. For provision, he gives a simple proof: we cannot live without food and drink, and Allah created our food and drink.

## From the Scholars (commentary)

**1. The first matter: the words of the original text, as quoted by Shaykh Ibn Baz**

> اعْلمْ ـ رَحِمَكَ اللهُ ـ أَنَّه يَجِبُ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ وَمُسْلِمَةٍ تَعَلُّمُ هذه المسائل الثلاث والْعَمَلُ بهنُّ "الأُولَى": أَنَّ اللهَ خَلَقَنَا وَرَزَقَنَا ولم يتركنا هملاً

Meaning: "Every Muslim, male and female, must learn these three matters and act upon them. The first: Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 29

**2. Why did Allah create us? Shaykh Ibn Baz**

> الله خلق الخلق ليعبدوه فلم يخلقهم هملاً، ولا سداً، ولا عبثاً.

Meaning: "Allah created His creation to worship Him. He did not create them without a purpose or for nothing."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 29

**3. The ayah Shaykh Ibn Baz cites: Surat adh-Dhariyat 51:56**

> وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ ٥٦

Meaning: "And I (Allâh) created not the jinn and mankind except that they should worship Me (Alone)." (*The Noble Qur'an*, Hilali-Khan). Arabic text: King Fahd Complex (Madinah) mushaf.

**4. How do we know Allah created us? Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen**

> ودليل ذلك أعني أن الله خلقنا سمعي وعقلي

Meaning: "The proof of this, I mean that Allah created us, comes from the revelation we hear and from reason."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 29

**5. The reason proof he points to: Surat at-Tur 52:35**

> أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ ٣٥

Meaning: "Were they created by nothing? Or were they themselves the creators?" (*The Noble Qur'an*, Hilali-Khan). Arabic text: King Fahd Complex (Madinah) mushaf.

**6. Not by himself, not by his parents, not by chance: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> فإن الإنسان لم يخلق نفسه ... ولم يخلقه أبوه ولا أمه ولا أحد من الخلق، ولم يكن ليأتي صدفة ... فتعين بهذا أن يكون الخالق هو الله وحده فلا خالق ولا آمر إلا الله

Meaning: "Man did not create himself … his father did not create him, nor his mother, nor anyone from the creation, and he did not come by chance … So it is clear from this that the Creator is Allah alone: there is no creator and no one who commands except Allah."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 29

**7. Allah provided for us: Shaykh al-Uthaymeen**

> فلأننا لا نعيش إلا على طعام وشراب، والطعام والشراب خلقه الله عز وجل

Meaning: "Because we cannot live without food and drink, and food and drink were created by Allah, the Mighty and Majestic."
Source: *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 30

**8. The ayah he cites for provision: Surat adh-Dhariyat 51:58**

> إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ ٥٨

Meaning: "Verily, Allâh is the All-Provider, Owner of Power, the Most Strong." (*The Noble Qur'an*, Hilali-Khan). Arabic text: King Fahd Complex (Madinah) mushaf.

**9. The seed we sow: Surat al-Waqi'ah 56:63–64, also cited by Shaykh al-Uthaymeen on p. 30**

> أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ ٦٣ ءَأَنتُمۡ تَزۡرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلزَّٰرِعُونَ ٦٤

Meaning: "Then tell Me about the seed that you sow in the ground. Is it you that make it grow, or are We the Grower?" (*The Noble Qur'an*, Hilali-Khan). Arabic text: King Fahd Complex (Madinah) mushaf.

## Say it like this (script)
1. "Who made you? Did you make yourself? No! Did Mummy and Daddy make you? No! Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said a person did not create himself, his parents did not create him, and he did not come by chance. Allah alone created us."
2. "Allah also **provided for us**. We cannot live without food and drink, and Allah created our food and drink. Allah is ar-Razzāq, the All-Provider."
3. "And Allah **did not leave us without a purpose**. Shaykh Ibn Baz said Allah created His creation to worship Him." Read the meaning of 51:56 together.
4. Worksheet: "Let's trace خَلَقَنَا and رَزَقَنَا, then match each Arabic phrase to its meaning (or write it), and finish the last activity." (Depending on the version: circle why Allah created us, "To worship Him", or fill in the missing word "drink" in "We cannot live without food and drink.")

## If your child asks…
- **"Did Mummy and Daddy make me?"** No. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said a person's father and mother did not create him, nor did anyone from the creation; the Creator is Allah alone (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 29).
- **"Did I just happen by accident?"** No. Shaykh al-Uthaymeen said a person did not come by chance (Shaykh al-Uthaymeen, *Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, p. 29).
- **"Why did Allah make us?"** To worship Him. Shaykh Ibn Baz said Allah created His creation to worship Him, and did not create them without a purpose or for nothing (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Abdul-Aziz Ibn Baz (Dar al-Masir, 1st ed.), p. 29), and Allah says so in Surat adh-Dhariyat 51:56.
- **"Where does my food come from?"** Allah created our food and drink, and we cannot live without them (*Sharh Thalathat al-Usul*, Shaykh Muhammad ibn Salih al-Uthaymeen (Dar al-Thurayya, 4th ed.), p. 30). Allah is the All-Provider (51:58).
- **Other questions about provision** (for example, how it is written for each person): "We will learn more later, in sha Allah." This guide does not answer them yet.

## Review game: Who Created It?
Put picture or word cards of food and drink in a basket: dates, water, bread, an apple, rice (fruit, plants and objects only; no animals or people). Your child pulls out a card and says: "Allah created it!" Then say together: "Allah created us, provided for us, and did not leave us without a purpose." (Shaykh al-Uthaymeen: food and drink were created by Allah, p. 30; there is no creator except Allah, p. 29.)

Optional extra: plant a seed in a cup and water it. As it sprouts over the week, read the meaning of 56:63–64: "Is it you that make it grow, or are We the Grower?"
