## Patterns
- Most kids' printables sell for $1.50–$8; bundles go for $5–$15.
- Almost every top listing shows a "sale" price that's 25–75% off.
- The best sellers are busy books, activity packs, flashcards and dua cards. They're simple and printable, and work for many ages.
- Faceless coloring books already exist (e.g. a 50-page faceless coloring book, $2.07), so buyers want them.

## Gap for us
- We found **no Etsy printable for Thalathat al-Usul for kids.** Comparable products are a physical
  Dar-us-Salam activity book, a free printable from Allamah Education, and adult workbooks on Amazon.
- None of the top listings make "sourced from the scholars, every hadith cited" a selling point.

## Suggested pricing for Product 1
- Workbook (30–40 pages): list at $12–$14 and run a regular sale near $6–$8, matching the market pattern.
- Later: a bundle (Usul + Arabic letters + duas) at $18–$25.
