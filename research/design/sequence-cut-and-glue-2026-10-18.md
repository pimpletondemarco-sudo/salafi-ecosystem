Patterns:
1. **Target on top, pieces strip at the bottom, one cut line between them** is the norm for single-page
   cut-and-glue (1412678027, 4509672450). Bigger sets move the pieces to their own page (1342634688,
   4352312895).
2. **Empty target boxes are the same size as the pieces**, so the child can see where each one goes
   (4509672450, 1342634688).
3. **Steps are numbered** and the target shows the order (numbered boxes, two rows of five).
4. Instructions are one line, verb first ("Cut, arrange and glue…", "Cut out the pictures and stick
   them in order").
5. Sellers show a **black-and-white version** and offer **laminate + Velcro** reuse.
6. Sequencing in Islamic shops is almost always wudu or salah steps drawn with people (see §5).
   **Ordering the matters of a book's matn on a path is not offered** by anyone in the 9 + 121 results.

  (shop 21723207, twice, 2026-03-27).
- **Content accuracy is noticed** even on hands-on pages: a 4★ review of a wudu cut-and-paste from the
  same shop as 1412678027 points out a wrongly spelled Shahada transliteration (listing 4328543105,

- Scissor skills (NHS OT pages, **search snippets only**, 2026-10-18): straight lines first, then
  corners, then curves; "start with thick bold cutting lines"; scissor skills "not fully developed until
  6 years of age"; by about 6 a child can cut shapes with curved and straight edges.

## 3. Rules for our page (from the complaints and the market)

**R1 — A glued piece never covers anything printed.**
- Each stone holds an **empty glue zone**: a rounded rectangle 0.15 in (4 mm) larger than the piece on
  every side, in a light tone (bone #e5e1d8 or 15% green tint), with nothing printed inside it
  except, optionally, the word "glue" in muted text that the piece will cover.
- The stone's **number sits outside the glue zone**: a 0.45 in green numbered circle on the path just
  before the stone, so it stays visible after gluing.
- No title, instruction, Arabic or source text within 0.25 in of a glue zone.

**R2 — Every piece is used; no extras.** Exactly 4 pieces for 4 stones. No decoy piece, no spare stone.
The answer key shows the finished page (answer key layout A, owner 2026-10-18).

**R3 — The finished page teaches.** Each piece carries the whole item: the fully voweled Arabic word
and its short English meaning (only from the verified Lesson 1 source). When all 4 are glued, the page
reads as a correct summary in the book's order. Pieces are printed **shuffled** on the strip (never 1-2-3-4).

**R4 — Cutting strip for ages 6–10.**
- One horizontal strip at the bottom of the page, **inside the 0.5 in margin**, separated from the
  stones by at least 0.6 in of white space.
- **Four rectangles side by side sharing their edges**, so the child makes only straight cuts: one long
  cut to free the strip, then three short cuts between pieces (or 5 cuts if edges are not shared).
  No curves, no rounded corners, no small notches (curves come later for 6-year-olds, per the OT snippets).
- **Piece size:** 1.75 in wide × 1.25 in tall (4.4 × 3.2 cm). Four in a row = 7.0 in, which fits the
  7.5 in live width of US Letter and the 7.27 in of A4 (12.7 mm margins). Never smaller than
  1.5 × 1.0 in: smaller pieces are hard to glue for a 6-year-old and the Arabic would shrink.
- **Cut lines: solid, 2 pt, dark grey (#4a4a4a).** Thick solid lines are easier to follow than thin
  dashes ("thick bold cutting lines"). Cut lines are functional, so they do not break the "no dashed
  lines as ornament" rule in `design-principles.md`; do not add any decorative dashed border.
- **Safe zone inside each piece: 0.15 in.** No Arabic letter, haraka or English text closer than
  0.15 in to a cut line, so a child cutting slightly inside the line never cuts off a vowel mark.
- Arabic on pieces: Noto Sans Arabic, fully voweled, about 30–34 px; English meaning 15–16 px Nunito.
  Every piece passes `check_arabic.py` (Letter and A4) like any other page.
- A small scissors icon (an object, allowed) at the start of the strip's top line. No hands.

**R5 — Instructions:** a numbered 1-2-3 line, verb first, 17–18 px, words not icons:
"1 Cut out the 4 pieces. 2 Put them in order. 3 Glue each one on its stone." (the designer may shorten;
the order words must match the lesson's verified wording).

**R6 — Ink and printing.** White page; stones as light-tone shapes (no dark fills, no gradients);
total tone under about 15% of the page. Check the page in greyscale: stones, numbers and pieces must
still read. Single-sided. A note in the teaching guide: "Regular paper works; cardstock makes the
pieces sturdier; a glue stick works better than liquid glue."

## 4. Recommendations for OUR page, ranked (keep / change / add)

**Keep**
1. The approved two-page lesson (Learn + Do), our header and footer panels, Baloo 2 / Nunito /
   Noto Sans Arabic, the "Source for parents" footer and "Answers: Answer Key, Lesson 1".

**Add (this page)**
2. **Path of 4 stones across the top ~55% of the activity area, read left to right in a gentle zigzag**
   (top-left → bottom-right), each stone with its glue zone (R1) and numbered circle. English-reading
   children follow 1→4 left to right; the numbers make the order explicit, so the right-to-left Arabic
   words inside the pieces are not confused with the path direction.
3. **End the path at the one small palm picture** (owner 2026-10-18: one small palm only). The palm
   counts as the page's single picture — do not add any other art. See `palm-spot-illustration-2026-10-18.md`.
4. **Cutting strip at the bottom** as R4, with the four pieces shuffled.
5. Answer key page: a small filled-in copy with all four pieces glued, plus the source line.
6. Black-and-white check of the whole page before posting to the HQ (R6).

**Make 3 versions (A/B/C) for the owner:**
- A: zigzag path, pieces strip at the bottom of the same page (market norm, 1412678027).
- B: straight row of 4 stones climbing like steps toward the palm, strip at the bottom.
- C: a 2 × 2 grid of stones with short arrows 1→2→3→4, strip at the bottom.
All three follow R1–R6. Never put pieces on the back of a page: double-sided printing fails at home.

**Change (do not do)**
7. Do not use Velcro-style photo mockups, laminated binders or hands in listing images (CLAUDE.md).
   Show the page flat, then a second flat render with the pieces glued in place (as 1412678027 shows
   its finished mosque), on a desk or clipboard template with no people.

## 5. Warnings: competitor patterns that break our rules (selling points for us)

- **Drawn people in sequencing:** wudu chart with "a male character and a female character"
  (1342634688, seen: faceless figures — still drawings of people); salah-step sequencing
  (1870849895); prophets sequencing cards described as "beautifully illustrated" (4381939460, not
  viewed); story sequencing pages full of drawn children (4509672450) and **real children's hands**
  in its photos.
- **Hands in mockups:** 4352312895 (a hand placing a masjid piece).
- **Unchecked text:** Shahada transliteration error reported by a buyer (4328543105, 2025-10-06).
- 1412678027 and its sister page use only buildings — the one competitor format that already fits our
  rules. Do not copy its silhouette idea; ours is a numbered path of words, not a picture puzzle.
