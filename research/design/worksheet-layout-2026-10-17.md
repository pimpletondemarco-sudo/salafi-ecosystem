## 1. Common patterns in the market

| Element | What the evidence shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **One skill per section, few sections** | Educator guides: one instruction set per section, predictable structure page to page; preschool: one skill per page (two if complementary); crowded pages with six problem types are called the main failure; ≤~8 items per skill. | L2 |
| **White space** | Empty space is a cue for where to answer; lowers load. Leave generous room after each task. | L2 |
| **Instructions** | Short, consistent visual cue system, ≤2 font styles, no all-caps for emerging readers. | L2 |
| **Write-in space** | ≥0.75 in per answer line for early writers; 3/4 in (gr.1), 5/8 in (gr.2). | L3, L4 |
| **Black-and-white / printer-friendly** | Several Islamic Etsy sets advertise black-and-white, "easy printing and reuse"; one says pages "print well on Letter size too as well as A4". A reviewer of another site praised sheets that "print cleanly without wasting ink or paper". | L5, L6 |
| **Letter + A4** | Islamic sellers state paper size in the title ("PDF Download A4 Size") or promise both. | L5 |
| **Answer keys** | Rare among Islamic Etsy listings; complaints elsewhere when keys mismatch. | L5, L7 |
| **Printer margins** | Home printers can't print in an edge zone of ~0.1–0.3 in; most laser/inkjets don't do borderless on plain paper. | L8 |

## 2. What buyers praise and complain about

- **Complaint (real buyer review, Islamic):** on a Holistic Muslimah Islamic activities library, a
  reviewer said some activities have quite large borders that slow the printer and use a lot of ink,
  and asked for smaller borders (paraphrase of snippet). (L1)
- **Praise (same seller):** "a complete package to start teaching my little ones about deen"
  (snippet). Another review asked for translations (English-only limited them). (L1)
- **General:** curricula criticised as "not very … printer friendly"; answer keys that don't match. (L7)
- Parents in our niche notice and praise "no faces" (tracing report S22).

## 3. Our page measured (2026-10-17)

| Item | Ours | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Full-width bone band (#e5e1d8), 228 px of 2112 (~1.15 in) tall: "The Three Fundamental Principles, Lesson 3", title "My Three Roots", Arabic الأُصُولُ الثَّلَاثَةُ in green | Clear, branded, uses the exact product name. Tall for a worksheet. |
| Name / Date | Two lines under header, 16px bold | Market-normal; good. |
| Sections | 3 activities, each with a green numbered circle, bold verb title + grey instruction | Clear and consistent. Matches "one task per section". |
| Instructions | Verb-first, one sentence, sentence case, grey #6b5b51 on white (~6.5:1 contrast) | Good. Small (16px) for self-reading 6-year-olds; parent reads aloud. |
| Footer | Bone band: "Source for parents:" quote (meaning) + real book, publisher, edition, printed page; SunnahSaplings wordmark | Unique in the market; strong trust signal. |
| Ink | Average coverage ≈ 5.5% (Letter) / 5.2% (A4), measured from PNG greyscale; ~22% of pixels non-white; bands = 17% of page area but light tint | Low — about a standard text page (ISO ~5%). Not an ink problem. |
| Edges | Header and footer bands bleed to the paper edge; page margin 0 | Home printers can't print the outer ~0.1–0.3 in (L8), so the bands will show uneven white edges or be scaled by "fit to page". Cosmetic risk. |
| Page number / lesson id | Lesson in header; no page number | Hard to re-sort loose pages in a binder or match to the answer key. |
| A4 vs Letter | Same layout, more vertical gap on A4 | Both fine. |

## 4. Recommendations for our page, ranked

**Keep**
1. **3 activities per page maximum**, each with a numbered circle, verb-first bold title and a one-sentence
   instruction. It already matches best practice; don't add a 4th.
2. **"Source for parents" footer** with the real book, publisher, edition and printed page. No competitor
   seen does this; make it a listing selling point ("every page cited").
3. White page, colour only in small accents; light ink use (~5.5%). Name and Date lines.
4. Exact product name in the header plus the Arabic title, fully voweled.

**Change**
5. **Pull the bands in from the paper edge**: keep all ink at least 0.3 in from every edge — e.g. a
   rounded light header panel inside the margin, or a thin rule instead of a band. Avoids clipped edges
   on home printers and answers the "big borders" complaint (L1). Highest impact for print quality.
6. **Shrink the header to ~0.8–0.9 in** (smaller title, same content). It frees ~0.3 in, which the
   tracing "write it yourself" box, the larger matching Arabic and the fill-in write line will need.
7. **Instruction size 17–18px** and keep them on their own line under the title when long, so a child
   who can read can follow them; don't use all-caps.
8. Check the page printed in greyscale: green dots/number circles must still read as dark (they do
   at #456f3a, but verify on a real B/W print).

**Add**
9. **Small page reference in the footer** (e.g. "Lesson 3 · Page 2") and, on activities with answers,
   "Answers: Answer Key p. X". Helps binder sorting and the parent finding answers.
10. **Plan writing space first**: every write-in answer ≥0.7 in tall and long enough for the word
    (see fill-in report). If the three activities no longer fit, move one to the next page rather
    than shrink writing space.
11. Listing copy (later, with mockups): say "black-and-white friendly, low ink, US Letter + A4,
    answer key with sources, no drawings of people or animals".

## 5. Warnings: competitor patterns that break our rules

- Character mascots and children drawn on headers and corners are common in Islamic packs; many
  sellers market them as "faceless". Drawings of people/animals of any kind break our rule.
- Decorative borders (lanterns are fine for us; animals, birds or people in borders are not) and
  heavy coloured frames — also the cause of the ink complaint (L1).
- Holiday-themed headers (non-Islamic holidays, mawlid) appear in the same categories; never use.
- Pages with Islamic facts and no source line; ours must always carry the source footer when a page
  contains religious content.
