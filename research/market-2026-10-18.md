# Competitor study: Product 1 "The Three Fundamental Principles" (2026-10-18)

**Data source:** Etsy Open API through the `mcp__etsy__*` connector. We used read-only calls only
(`search_listings`, `get_shop`, `get_listing_images`, `get_reviews_by_listing`, `get_reviews_by_shop`).
We did not touch our own shop (SunnahSaplings, shop_id 68627681). All data was pulled on **2026-10-18**.
In this file "API" means the Etsy Open API v3 on that date. Each number gives its endpoint and listing or shop id.

**What the API can and cannot tell us** (read this before relying on the numbers):
- The API gives **no sales per listing**. As proxies we use `num_favorers`, `views` and the shop-wide
  `transaction_sold_count` / `review_count`.
- Prices are **list prices**. Sale discounts don't show up. Some shops advertise them in their announcement, e.g. MinsaArts says
  "flat 70% off all educational printables" (`get_shop` 31821268).
- The `count` values from `search_listings` look smaller than what etsy.com shows. Treat them only as relative
  signals of demand and competition.
- `get_reviews_by_listing` returned 0–5 reviews even for big listings, so most review analysis below uses
  `get_reviews_by_shop` (100 most recent reviews per shop).
- Prices in other currencies are converted to **approximate USD (our estimate, not from the API)**.
- We did not look at competitor images, only their metadata (count, size, alt text). The faces/animals flags below come from
  **listing descriptions and titles**.

## 3. What buyers praise and complain about

Source: `get_reviews_by_shop` limit 100 for shops 31821268, 36857332, 8198311, 20205854, 25884639, 57491818,
22717843, 38774185, 55494720, plus `get_reviews_by_listing` 1679381264, 2026-10-18. Below we put the reviews in our own words,
with the listing id each one came from.

**Praise (what to keep doing)**
- **Instant, easy download and printing.** This is the most common comment in every shop.
- **A guide or lesson plan included.** MinsaArts buyers on listing 4360845151 said the lesson plan left "zero guess work"
  and valued the guide on how to use each section. This is the strongest single signal for our teaching guide.
- **Two paper sizes.** An IslamicArtPrintables buyer praised the two size options (listing 1729627527).
- **Arabic shown with its meaning** helps children learn (IslamicArtPrintables 1492480565).
- **One topic per sheet**, so a parent can choose where to start (MinsaArts 4360845151).
- **Laminate and reuse.** Buyers often mention laminating, putting pages in binders and using them in class.
- Clear, colourful, "simple and beautiful" design (36857332, 25884639).

**Complaints (what to avoid)**
- **No instructions** for how to use the pages: a 3★ review on Wuffyland 1673255058.
- **Content errors**: labels that don't match the pictures (1★, Wuffyland 1667674032); typos (4★, IslamicPrintabless 4327018045);
  a missing Arabic letter and letters out of order (4★, MuslimJoy 1146476317).
- **Made by someone who doesn't know Arabic**: English sounds didn't match the Arabic letters, so the buyer didn't use it
  (2★, IslamicArtPrintables 4321282635).
- **"Seems AI generated"**: the buyer worried about accuracy (IslamicPrintabless 4321057693). This matches our owner's own
  rule against an AI look.
- **Download problems**: "I don't get my book" (1★, MinsaArts 1679381264, a file hosted on an external link because of its size);
  "only getting the first letter" (1★, MinsaArts 4539223805).
- **Wrong size**: an A4-only file left white space when printed on another paper size (DitifyPages 4304679390).
- **Cut-outs too small** (Growinglittletots 1253418680); bookmarks "too small, too expensive" (1★, PrimaryIlm 1095632241).
- **Requests**: transliteration (PrimaryIlm 966736134); Quran Arabic first, then transliteration, then meaning
  (IslamicPrintabless 1888939311); French, German and Arabic versions (57491818 listings 1905325067 and 4463762240;
  55494720 listings 1889054143 and 4321057693).

## 5. Gaps that fit our rules

1. **The topic itself is empty.** There is no kids' printable for the Three Fundamental Principles. "three fundamental principles" gives 10 results,
   all physical adult books. "thalathat al usul", "usool ath thalatha" and "three principles islam kids" give 0 (`search_listings`).
   The closest item is ilmCards' grave-questions flashcards (1340155377, £2.99).
2. **Kids' aqeedah products are thin and small.** "aqeedah worksheets" gives 1 result and "tawheed worksheets" 4. The digital kids' aqeedah
   items we found are 1-page sheets ($1.75, 4387129240), a game ($7.99), or listings with few or no sales
   (LearnWithSulayman 8 sold; AqidahJunior 2 sold; `get_shop`).
3. **Answer key plus teaching guide is rare.** Of the 618 digital listings, 62 mention an answer key and 30 mention a guide or lesson plan.
   Only **11 mention both** (description text search). The best of them is DitifyPages 4347953176, with 5 answer pages, A4 only,
   and animals. Buyers praise guides (MinsaArts reviews) and complain when there are no instructions (Wuffyland). Our full teaching
   guide answers both.
4. **Real sourcing is almost absent.** Only 8 of 618 listings cite a hadith collection, and all of them are 0–31 days old
   with 0 favourites (e.g. 4576889107, 4597129257, 4597942978, 4596867798). So "sourced" listings are just appearing.
   **No listing cites a scholar's book, edition and page.** The few that name scholars do it in a disclaimer
   (e.g. 1860489884 "consult with a qualified scholar"; 4568426145 "not taught by a shaykh"). Our citations on each page are a
   clear difference. They also answer the review worries about accuracy and an AI look.
5. **No living beings, done well.** Many competitors draw animals or people. Others sell "faceless" pictures
   (11 listings mention "faceless", e.g. 4598024051, 4598028811, 4531519867 "faceless animals ... 100% halal").
   No competitor in this niche offers aqeedah pages with **no living beings at all** (plants, masjids,
   patterns, calligraphy) and says so.
6. **Both sizes are not standard.** Only 227 of 618 listings mention both Letter and A4. Strong listings ship one size only
   (4321057693 Letter only; 4347953176 A4 only, with a buyer complaint). We ship both.
7. **Arabic done right.** Buyers complain about non-Arabic-speaker errors and ask for transliteration. Our fully voweled Arabic
   (King Fahd Complex text for ayat), simple transliteration in the guide, and the `check_arabic.py` check answer this.

## 6. Pricing

**Market** (`search_listings` USD-priced digital listings collected above, 2026-10-18, list prices):
- All USD digital kids' Islamic listings (n = 333): median **$4.99**, middle half $3.00–$6.99.
- USD listings with "workbook / curriculum / activity book / bundle" in the title (n = 123): median **$5.77**,
  middle half $3.99–$8.99, top $59.00.
- Strongest comparable full products: MinsaArts busy book **$12.00** (35 pp, best seller, with a shop-wide sale
  announced); MuslimJoy curriculum **$14.99** (100 pp); UpperEastBranding **$14.00** (200+ pp, new);
  LearnWithSulayman aqeedah **£12.49** (≈$16.60 est., almost no sales); physical adult Usul workbook **$17.99**.

**Suggested price for Product 1** (first release: lessons 1–3, about 25–35 student pages + answer key + teaching guide, Letter + A4):
- **List at $12.99, with a launch sale of about 30% (≈ $8.99) for the first 2–4 weeks.**
- Reasons:
  1. It sits in the same band as the best-selling full products ($12–$15). A sale makes it feel like the $6–$9
     workbooks that most buyers pick.
  2. Nobody else offers the topic, the answer key, the teaching guide and the citations together. That supports pricing above the $5.77 median.
  3. We are a new shop with 0 reviews (per the manager's brief of 2026-10-18; we did not call the API on our own shop on purpose).
     Going above $15 before we have reviews is risky. LearnWithSulayman at £12.49 has 8 sales with weak presentation.
  4. Later, when all lessons are done: the full workbook at $16.99–$19.99, and a bundle with other products at $22–$25. This matches
     the earlier plan in research/market-2026-10-16.md.
- This is a small change from the 2026-10-16 scan ($12–14 list, $6–8 sale). The API data supports a slightly higher sale floor
  (≈$8.99), because the answer key and guide put us above the median workbook.

## 7. Actions for the team

1. Listing: use the title and 13 tags above. Show 10 images, landscape ≈2700×2025, one per section, with alt text on every image
   (like the best seller). Add a "What's inside" image, a "Every answer sourced: book, scholar, page" image, and a
   "No drawings of people or animals" badge image (flat-lay mockups only, no hands).
2. Deliver the files on Etsy itself (no external hosting link), in separate Letter and A4 PDFs, named clearly. This
   avoids the download complaints.
3. Put a one-page "How to use this workbook" at the front. Buyers complain when it is missing.
4. Keep cut-outs and tracing large. Add simple transliteration in the guide. Show ayat in Arabic first, then the meaning.
5. Re-run this study after launch and track our listing's `views` and `num_favorers` weekly through `get_listing`.
