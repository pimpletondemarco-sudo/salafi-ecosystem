# Market fit check: Product 1 vs competition (2026-10-16)

Sources: Etsy category and listing pages via web search, 2026-10-16. Snapshots only; check live pages before relying on numbers.

## Where we match or beat the market
- Topic gap: no Etsy kids' printable for Thalathat al-Usul found. Closest tawheed items are a $1.75 single tawheed worksheet and a "Little Muslim Aqeedah Workbook" (ages 4–8).
- Answer key + parent teaching guide together: not found on any Etsy aqeedah/tawheed listing (a Ramadan activity book has an answer key; a free blog set has a lesson guide).
- Real citations (scholar, book, edition, page) and Quran text from the King Fahd Complex: no competitor advertises this.

## Where we are behind
- Look: top sellers use colourful cover art and many cute illustrations; buyers praise "the illustrations" in reviews. Our draft is plain line art with a text-heavy footer.
- Size: best sellers are 30-page books, busy books, or big bundles. We have 1 page so far.
- Listing assets: competitors show 5–10 mockup images, page previews, sometimes a certificate. We have none yet.
- Reading level: our coloring-page footer is adult-level text; for ages 6–10 it should move to the answer key / teaching guide.
- Many competitors draw faceless people and animals; we draw no living beings at all, so our art must win with trees, masjids, patterns and bold colour.

## Actions
1. Coloring pages: drop the long citation from the student page; put a short "From Sharh Thalathat al-Usul, p. 42" line and move the full quote to the answer key.
2. Make a coloured cover and a consistent sapling mascot (a plant, not a creature).
3. First release: 25–35 pages (lessons 1–3 worksheets + coloring + tracing + certificate) + answer key + teaching guide, so it compares with the $10 30-page books.
4. Price: list $12–14 with launch sale near $7–9, as in the first market scan.
5. Listing: 7+ mockups, "what's inside" image, a "sourced from the scholars" badge image.
